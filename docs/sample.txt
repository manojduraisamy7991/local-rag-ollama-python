Acme Corp Employee Handbook (sample)

Leave policy: Full-time employees get 24 days of paid leave per year. Unused leave up to 10 days can be carried over to the next year.

Work from home: Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

Refund policy: Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, only store credit is offered.

Support hours: Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM IST, at support@acme.example.
